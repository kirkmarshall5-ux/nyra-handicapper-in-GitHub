# October 21 prospective freeze acceptance checklist (research only)

Before first post, verify the correct date, track, complete race card, provider (DRF or Brisnet), and scratch list. A successful page load is not evidence that the current card loaded: visually verify race count, runners and race dates. If stale or empty, **HOLD** and do not certify that card.

After the complete card is verified, use **Freeze All Models** before the first actual off time. Capture the on-screen confirmation and save/export the immutable recorder data, if available. Keep the original files unchanged. Record the track, race numbers, model names/versions, PP provider, freeze timestamp with time zone, and snapshot/record IDs. Do not replace or overwrite a frozen record with a later update.

For each race, verify the snapshot is independently anchored before **actual off time**, not merely scheduled post time. Preserve both PASS and ACTION race predictions; PASS races are descriptive research only and excluded from action-only betting summaries. Keep DRF and Brisnet comparisons separate. If any horse lacks a ranking, preserve that omission; do not reconstruct it after the race.

After racing, obtain official charts for scratches, actual starters, actual surface, actual off time, DNF/dead heat and finishing order. Grade only when the final field, full ranked list, official result and independent timestamp are reconciled. Otherwise mark **HOLD** with a reason. Never use retrospective replay as prospective evidence. No ROI until prices and tickets are independently verified.

**October 16 remains a diagnostic-only nine-race archive**, not a prospective test. This checklist does not change production behavior, scoring, betting or deploy any code.
