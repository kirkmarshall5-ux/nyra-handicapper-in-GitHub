# Brisnet Premium Plus adapter

Independent format detection preserves the existing Ultimate and DRF parsers. The model weights and provider validation gates are unchanged. Uploads remain local to the browser.

Verified source: Belmont October 16, 2026, 20 pages, 9 races, 79 runner headers/career records and 311 historical lines. Independent Poppler word positions agreed with PDF.js extraction on all 1,866 E1/E2/LP/1c/2c/Speed cells, including blanks. Four historical lines lack E2 or LP evidence; null is retained. Source PDFs and full cards are not included in this public repository.

Premium Plus omits Ultimate's RR/CR columns. Right-aligned figures sometimes share a PDF text item. Whitespace tokens and column ends separate those values; unresolved numeric positions reject the candidate. Three-digit figures and ten-plus post positions have regression coverage.

Native figures live under ratings.provider=BRISNET and ratings.lines. Brisnet Speed is not Beyer. Beyer and Timeform inputs remain empty. FTS pace and style remain Unknown. Original model PASS gates remain until provider-specific scoring is validated. Workouts and trainer angles are not certified structured inputs.

Use the existing PDF upload control; it automatically detects Premium Plus, Ultimate or DRF. Saved cards recognize all three parser IDs. Verify current scratches and conditional entries separately. The historical index/Scratch Watch appendix never supplies current scratches.
