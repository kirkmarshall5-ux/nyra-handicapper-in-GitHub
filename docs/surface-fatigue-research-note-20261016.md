# Surface, energy distribution and fatigue — research note

Recorded October 16, 2026, at Kirk Marshall's request.

## Observation to carry into testing

Different track surfaces and conditions may affect how quickly individual horses tire and how effectively they sustain speed. A horse's performance on one surface should not automatically establish its suitability for another. Treat these as hypotheses to evaluate, not universal fatigue penalties or established causal conclusions.

## Evidence to retain

- Scheduled surface and verified actual racing surface separately; record any change and the time it became known.
- Track and named course where known: dirt/main, inner turf, outer/Widener turf, Tapeta or other verified synthetic type. Do not treat all synthetic formulations or all turf courses as identical.
- Going/condition, distance, race date and available weather, maintenance, rail/layout and bias evidence. Missing context remains unknown.
- Each horse's source-tagged historical E1, E2, LP and Speed, source dates and distance/call definitions; retain running style, trip and layoff context when verified.
- Same-surface versus surface-switch history, with separate dirt/turf/synthetic sprint/route cohorts. Examine repeat patterns within a horse before generalizing across a field or track.
- Whether competitive early pace is followed by weaker or stronger late pace and actual finishing performance, relative to comparable races and opposition.

## Interpretation limits

E1/E2/LP measure performance over defined race segments; they do not directly measure physiological fatigue or prove that a surface caused a late fade. Different pace pressure, trip, fitness, class, distance and condition can produce similar patterns. LP segments vary with race distance, and pace providers are not interchangeable. An E2-to-LP comparison must not be described as elapsed-time deceleration or a direct fatigue measurement.

Do not infer a day-wide bias from one result or assume that Tapeta always tires horses more or less than dirt or turf. Verify actual surface and race-specific conditions before assigning a result to a cohort. Belmont October 16 R3 remains a scheduled-Turf versus screenshot-Synthetic discrepancy pending race-specific verification.

## Test queue

1. Keep V4.4c, Contender, MIX1, all native versions and BRIS-PACE-CONTEXT-v1 fixed while collecting this evidence.
2. Track repeated late-performance changes by horse, track/course, actual surface, going and distance category. Report sample size, missingness and confounding factors.
3. Propose any horse-by-surface suitability or fatigue-related adjustment as a separately versioned candidate. Change one feature at a time and compare against unchanged parents on identical eligible races.
4. Test on later, independently anchored pre-off forecasts. Do not promote an adjustment from one race or retrospectively rerank an off-turf race and call it prospective.

No scoring change, fatigue penalty, synthetic weighting profile, probability conversion or betting approval is introduced by this note.
