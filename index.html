How Contender ranks

Contender V2 combines relative field scores using frozen coefficients for recent Beyers, surface and distance form, Timeform Early and Late pace, race pressure, career records, jockey and trainer records, layoffs, workouts, verified trip comments and prior opposition. It also uses a surface/distance matched Beyer trend when at least three comparable starts are verified. Missing features are neutral. DRF · Finisher
How Finisher ranks

Fixed weights: recent speed 60%, same-surface/distance speed 25%, Late pace 15%. Inputs use field percentiles from 0 to 100. Recent speed uses the latest five starts at 50/25/15/7/3, normalized for available figures. Matching speed uses the latest five proof rows, within 365 days and one furlong, with a 90-day decay half-life. Verified DRF fractional distances are decoded separately from the control. Missing optional inputs stay neutral at 50 without redistributing weights. First-time starters and runners without verified recent speed remain unranked; partial fields show PASS. Brisnet uses native Speed and matched LP. Exact score ties use horse name. V4.4c remains the control.

Locked Contender V2 rules

Ranks the eligible field for top-three finishing potential; displays the four highest-rated candidates. Contender uses frozen feature weights and a matched Beyer-trend adjustment. Its score is a relative rating, not a calibrated chance of winning.
Figures and suitability. Latest Beyer, weighted recent five, best of latest three, same-surface figures and same-surface/distance figures. Recent-five weights are 50%, 25%, 15%, 7% and 3%, normalized for fewer career starts only when the actual-start sequence is verified; otherwise use the latest verified figure.
Pace and records. Early/Late pace and pressure-related late fit; source-backed surface, distance and placing records. Surface/distance figures use verified rows within 365 days; distance matches are within half a furlong. Record features require at least three starts.
Connections and readiness. Verified annual jockey/trainer counts with at least 25 starts, otherwise qualifying local counts. Rates are smoothed as (wins + 2) ÷ (starts + 20). Readiness uses days since the latest verified start and workout frequency/rank during the past 30 days.
Relative scoring. Available factors are compared within the eligible field on a scale from −1 to +1, then combined with locked coefficients. Missing or constant factors contribute zero. Higher totals rank first; negative scores are valid. Scores display to three decimal places; the unrounded score determines rank.
Matched trend. Add 0.10 × the field-relative Beyer trend only with a verified actual-start window and at least three comparable starts among the latest five: same surface, within half a furlong, and within 365 days. Unverified trend adds zero.
Source and PASS rules. Preserve V4.4c full-field PASS decisions and exclude scratched or unconfirmed conditional entrants. PASS rows are provisional rated-runner diagnostics. Trip and opposition features require separately verified evidence; the current upload path leaves them neutral. Missing Contender source evidence requires a PP re-upload.
Locked for testing. Coefficients were trained for top-three contention, not directly calibrated for race wins. Estimated win chances and Fair Odds remain experimental. V4.4c remains the comparison control; probability and wagering approval remain pending.

Why a fallback?

Browsers can block one website from reading another website directly (CORS). This is a browser security rule, not a data problem. V2 therefore includes a no-server fallback: open the NYRA race page, select/copy the race text, paste it here, and parse it locally.
