Adaptive evidence model

What the Handicap Rating Knows

Missing race information is unknown — not zero and not an invented 50. As racing history becomes sparse, the rating shifts toward available workouts, pedigree and trainer situation evidence. Confidence measures the amount of direct evidence and is separate from the Handicap Rating.

V4 adaptive DRF extraction

V4 recognizes TimeformUS Early/Late, Beyer figures, trainer situation stats, workouts, sire/dam pedigree and running-line comments. Missing race figures stay N/A. For first-time and lightly raced horses, available works, pedigree and trainer evidence receive more weight instead of inventing missing performance data. It also scans for a small set of potentially useful trainer/class/form angles and explains why each one was flagged.

Why a fallback?

Browsers can block one website from reading another website directly (CORS). This is a browser security rule, not a data problem. V2 therefore includes a no-server fallback: open the NYRA race page, select/copy the race text, paste it here, and parse it locally.
